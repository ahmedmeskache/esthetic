Good to know

Questions people usually ask.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

You don't have to figure it out — that's our job. Book a consultation (it's included, no pressure) and we'll map your skin and recommend the smallest effective next step.

Are these painful?

Most of them are surprisingly comfortable. HydraFacial, peels and LED feel soothing. Microneedling gets numbed first and most clients describe it as "more weird than painful."

Is there any downtime?

Most of the menu has zero — walk out and carry on with your day. A few advanced options may cause light redness for a few hours, and we'll tell you before we start.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes, and it's common. HydraFacial plus LED, or a peel followed by dermaplaning later on — we'll build a sequence and space everything out.

What's the cancellation policy?

Things come up — just let us know at least 24 hours ahead and we'll reschedule you with zero hassle.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us what's been bothering you. We'll take it from there.
