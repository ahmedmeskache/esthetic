Our Story

Ten years. Same rule: listen first.

We built a clinic that doesn't upsell, doesn't rush, and doesn't pretend. That's it — that's the whole strategy.

Est. 2015 Zamalek · Cairo 3 specialists 8,000+ clients

The Glow started with a frustration anyone with problem skin in Cairo knows well: clinics that sell you a treatment before looking at you. Fix the spots, sell the peel, take the money — repeat.

So in 2015 we opened a different kind of room. One treatment bed, one founder, and one unbreakable rule — the consultation comes first, and the recommendation has to survive honest questions. If a simple routine solves your problem, that's what we say.

Year on year the equipment improved and the team grew, but the opening thirty minutes of every visit still look the same. We ask questions. We listen. Then we touch anything.

“Skin doesn't heal under pressure. It heals under attention.”

— Dana, founder
